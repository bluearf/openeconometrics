# Whole-group replay conditional logit

`clogit` can fit a replayable `Dataset` while keeping the total observation and group tables on owned disk storage. It evaluates the same exact conditional likelihood as the native in-memory estimator, conditioning on each group's observed number of positive outcomes. It does not split a group's likelihood into row-independent likelihoods or replace its denominator by a sampled approximation.

The adapter reuses the native float64 Torch elementary-symmetric-function recursion, including the optimized one-positive/one-negative group cases. Informative groups are packed into bounded complete-group blocks. A group can cross arbitrarily many source partitions: reader size and complete-group numerical size are planned separately. The largest individual group and conditional recursion width must fit the preallocation workspace plan. Thus arbitrarily many modest-size groups are supported without a total-row ceiling, while a group whose exact recursion needs too much workspace is explicitly refused. Recursion work still grows with group size and the smaller of its positive/negative counts.

Groups with no outcome variation are dropped and counted. Covariates are globally encoded and centered within complete groups; absorbed or collinear regressors are omitted after full-source TSQR screening. Group weights must be constant across every retained row of a group. Frequency weights replicate groups, while importance/sampling weights scale group contributions. Offsets, categorical covariates and missing policies preserve the native contract.

Conventional covariance uses the global observed information. Robust covariance uses complete group scores, with frequency replication semantics and the group small-sample factor. OPG uses the native group-score convention. One/two-way declared clusters use within-group-centered per-observation scores summed across all records on disk; split groups receive the established warning. Indefinite multiway meat is projected to PSD in the original centered regressor coordinates before the reporting transform. Conditional null likelihood, LR/Wald tests, information criteria and pseudo-R² use the complete sample.

All numerical blocks, derivative factors and SQLite caches are budgeted. Disk stores complete group records and means; it must have enough free capacity for the source and metadata. Source identity and effective physical-row fingerprints are rechecked before returning. A reporting sample of at most 400 rows does not determine the fit. This is bounded workspace, not a claim that every group size or every data volume runs quickly on every machine. There is no independent-observation GPU-iteration claim.

Tests cover all declared covariance/weight combinations, mixed group recursion cases, categorical/missing inputs, absorbed/collinear covariates, one/two-way split clusters, row/group counts, parameter units and input partition invariance, CPU context restoration, source mutation, separation and resource refusal. An independent enumeration of all admissible response configurations verifies a small-group fitted likelihood. Results round-trip through JSON and use the normal publication-table LaTeX exporter.
