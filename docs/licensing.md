# Licensing and commercial services

OpenEconometrics uses **Apache-2.0** for its original software. This is the
project's licensing choice for the Python SDK and local workbench, including
the desktop application, chart package and agent integration. The authoritative
terms are in [LICENSE](../LICENSE); this document explains the product direction
and does not add restrictions to those terms.

The choice supports adoption in research, teaching and commercial workflows,
including integration into other software. Apache-2.0 permits commercial use,
modification and redistribution subject to its conditions, and includes an
express contributor patent grant. It does not require downstream modifications
to be open source. Others may offer competing products or hosted services using
the software. A separate commercial license from this project is not required
merely because someone uses Apache-licensed code commercially.

## Free software and paid services

The community SDK and local workbench are intended to remain freely available
under Apache-2.0, including their analysis methods and basic result exports.
The initial commercial direction is to offer services around that software:

| Offering | Potential paid value |
| --- | --- |
| Managed cloud | Operated storage, synchronization, backups and shared projects |
| Teams | Managed collaboration, access administration and project history |
| Institutions | Deployment, integration, training and contracted support |
| Research services | Commissioned method development and reproducibility assistance |

These are product directions, not a claim that every feature is implemented or
that subscriptions, prices or service guarantees are currently available.
Any future hosted-service subscription or support agreement governs the
contracted service; it does not revoke Apache-2.0 rights in the software.
Providing the same services independently remains permitted by the code license.

No separate proprietary edition or dual-license program is established by this
document. Any future separately licensed module needs an explicit scope and
rights review before publication, including contributor and dependency rights.
Changing a later release's license would not revoke rights already granted in
earlier Apache-2.0 releases.

## Contributions, notices and other rights

Contributions intentionally submitted for inclusion follow Apache-2.0 section 5
unless expressly stated otherwise or covered by a separate agreement. Copyright
remains with the respective rights holders. This policy does not introduce a
copyright assignment or a new contributor agreement. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

Redistributions must comply with Apache-2.0, including retaining required
license, attribution and notice information and identifying changed files.
[NOTICE](../NOTICE) and [THIRD_PARTY.md](../THIRD_PARTY.md) record distribution
boundaries. Third-party dependencies, fonts, models, adapted assets and external
research data retain their own licenses or permissions; the project license
does not replace those terms or establish missing redistribution rights.

Apache-2.0 does not grant general rights to the project's trade names or marks.
The license's permitted origin descriptions and notice reproduction still apply.
This statement does not claim that a trademark is registered.
