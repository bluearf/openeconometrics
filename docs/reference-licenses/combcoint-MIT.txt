The Bayer-Hanck null distributions in
src/openecon/econometrics/unitroot/bayer_hanck_null.xz are a re-encoding
(values rounded to multiples of 1e-4) of the matrix ``null_dist`` in
R/sysdata.rda of the CRAN package combcoint 0.2.0
(https://cran.r-project.org/src/contrib/Archive/combcoint/combcoint_0.2.0.tar.gz,
sha256 2d7986b6779f87e172f2bcb1f3bfd70116d24f4a0a3129d126062c729ed5753c),
which equals, to float32 precision, NullDistr.dta of the authors' Stata module
bayerhanck (Christian Bayer and Christoph Hanck). combcoint is distributed
